    Interface Thermochemistry

    Rigid-rotor / harmonic-oscillator thermochemistry with Grimme's modified treatment of low-lying modes (mRRHO), matching xtb's own print_thermo. Every energy is in Hartree so the numbers line up with src/reference/fixtures/*.json.
