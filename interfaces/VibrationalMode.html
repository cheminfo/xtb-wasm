    cartesianDisplacement: Float64Array

    eigenvector

    eigenvector: Float64Array

    Raw mass-weighted eigenvector of the projected Hessian, length 3 · atoms. Already unit-normalized by the eigensolver, so it is stored exactly as the engine returns it. Intensities, reduced masses and Raman activities are all defined against THIS vector, never against a rescaled one.
